The tools

Three tools make the pages, one look. Everything runs in your browser; a guide saved to the team library reaches the others and, once it's ticked Published, the website.

  • Event GuideThe week's events from the Sheet as day pages for the carousel. Pick the week, check the dates, export.
  • Guide StudioEvent Guide Picks and What's New carousels from the weekly docs. Save to the team, tick Published for the website.
  • Tape TypePost covers, inside pages and video covers with the house tape headline.

The week

Events go in the Sheet and get ticked Approved. Stories go in the two docs. The docs are pasted into Guide Studio with Import copy; the Sheet's week is picked in the Event Guide. Export, post. A guide ticked Published and saved to the team is on the website at its next build.
